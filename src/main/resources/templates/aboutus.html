About HR Synergy (Ansh-HRMS)

Digitizing HR Operations & Empowering Organizations

Project Overview

HR Synergy is a cutting-edge digital Human Resource Management System designed to convert traditional paper-heavy operations into a seamless digital experience. It automates workforce management while enhancing employee engagement and eliminating manual inefficiencies.

Traditional System Issues

  • Manual sorting of paper job applications.
  • Error-prone manual data entry.
  • Limited accessibility and lack of real-time monitoring.

Proposed HR Synergy Solution

  • Automated candidate registration & CV management.
  • Centralized, cloud-backed secure database (MySQL).
  • Real-time analytics and 24/7 online portal access.

Technical Architecture

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap Backend: Java (Spring Boot) Database: MySQL IDE: Eclipse
